# Video rules (Flip the Switch hackathon)

Deliverable: "a short demo video of the workflow running" (devtoolsacademy.com/hackathons/flip-the-switch, Submission). No hard
length is stated on either hackathon page; "short" is a soft limit. The owner set the target at 3 to 4 minutes on Nov 7.
Judging: Use of Switch 30, Technical execution 25, Creativity and impact 25, Presentation 20 (DevTools Academy page).
AI tools are allowed ("You should understand what you submit and be able to explain how it works").
House rules: every number from the repo's eval scripts or a cited source shown on screen; real UI only; synthetic data and
scripted presses labelled; sped-up footage labelled; no credits in the film.
