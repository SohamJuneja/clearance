# Clearance site: brief

## Subject
Clearance is the approval layer for teams of people and AI agents working in Switch rooms. When an agent tries something risky
(a refund, a merge, a deploy, a customer email), the call is held, a native Switch card goes to the right person, and the person
who asked can't be the one who approves. A gate holds the credentials, so the agent can't go around it. Every decision lands on
a hash-chained ledger. Built for the Flip the Switch hackathon, Track 01, team dellfargo.

## Audience
Hackathon judges (Switch's engineers, DevTools people) reading fast, then developers and eng leads who'd run agents on real money.
Technical, sceptical of AI hype, impressed by evidence and by restraint.

## The one job
In ten seconds: "the person who asks can't be the one who approves", and that it's real (real agent, real Mattermost, measured).
Then send them to the demo video and the repo.

## Primary CTA
Watch the demo (3 min). Secondary: read the code on GitHub, read the results.

## The subject's world
Approval forms in triplicate, carbonless copies (white, canary, pink), rubber stamps and ink, signatures, two-person rule
(two keys), customs and air-traffic "clearance", cheque clearing, chain of custody, a ledger book, hash chains, redaction bars,
request numbers (CLR-0001), rupee amounts, a gate barrier. Chat rooms with cards and buttons.
How things move here: a stamp comes down hard and fast and leaves ink; a form slides out of a tray; a barrier drops and stops dead;
a ledger line is typed on and can't be changed. Sounds: a stamp's thud, a typewriter, a turnstile click (sound off by default).

## Dials
CRAZINESS 8, MOTION 7, DENSITY 4.

## Hard constraints
- One static page, no server: plain HTML, CSS and JS, libraries pinned (GSAP, Lenis), so it can go on GitHub Pages, any static
  host, or a shareable link. Lives in `site/`.
- Content that must appear: the one-line idea; the workflow (Priya asks, agent runs, gate holds, card opens, Priya refused,
  Sam decides, ledger); the real card screenshot and the Desk; the measured results with their caveats; how it uses Switch;
  limits; links to video, repo, results. Every number comes from README/RESULTS (generated by eval/), never typed fresh.
- Honest labels: synthetic data says synthetic, scripted presses say scripted.
- Quality floor: 360px up, keyboard, reduced motion, contrast, no layout shift. Lighthouse mobile perf 85+.
- Deadline: tonight.
