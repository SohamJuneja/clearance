## How far to trust this

- **A blank is not a no.** Code search has gaps: it returned nothing for "drill" in a repo called Blackout, or for "inbox" in Fleet Room, and those may simply not be in its index. Anything in the right-hand column above is unread.
- **I read fragments.** The brief allowed `gh search` only, so I didn't open whole READMEs or clone anything. Reading the two or three flagged repos would take a person a few minutes.
- **The search misses entries.** Switchyard, PromiseKeeper, Precedent and Lead Desk from PLAN 16.2 didn't appear in the 243, so that list is a floor. PLAN's Nov 5 figure of 180 repos came from a different method and can't be compared with it.
- **Submissions are private.** There's no public gallery (PLAN 16), so anything not on GitHub is invisible here.
